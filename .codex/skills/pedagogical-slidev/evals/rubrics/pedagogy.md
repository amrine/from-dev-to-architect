# Eval rubric: Pedagogy

Score each item `pass`, `issue`, or `critical`.

- Learner profile and performance gap are explicit.
- Objectives use observable verbs and conditions.
- Every objective maps to evidence, activity, and slides.
- The sequence uses appropriate prerequisites and progressive disclosure.
- A whole professional task or credible scenario anchors the learning.
- The learner retrieves, predicts, compares, explains, or practises where needed.
- Worked examples or guided completion precede independent reasoning when needed.
- The storyline exposes problem, need, choice, compromise, implementation, and proof.
- The deck closes the opening scenario and includes bounded transfer.
- Each slide has one main objective.
- Notes add oral teaching value and match reveals and transitions.
