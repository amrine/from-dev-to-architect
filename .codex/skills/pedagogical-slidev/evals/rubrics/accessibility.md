# Eval rubric: Accessibility

- Meaning is not conveyed by color alone.
- Text and code remain readable at desktop and small viewport sizes.
- Headings and links describe their purpose.
- Diagrams have a reading direction, labels, and a textual conclusion.
- Progressive reveals are not required to understand a timed or hidden fact.
- Fully revealed states remain coherent.
- Notes provide an oral alternative to visual relationships.
- Exercises state expected output and success criteria.
- No secrets or private operational data appear in slides or notes.
