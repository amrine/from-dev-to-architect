# Eval rubric: Slidev quality

- `slides.md` and imported pages are valid and discoverable.
- Week and ticket navigation is complete and coherent.
- Generated zones are stable and unrelated manual content is preserved.
- Every presented slide has one valid presenter note.
- Notes are placed in imported pages and generated markers are not parsed as notes.
- ADR paths or missing-ADR warnings appear in ticket sections.
- Design tokens and existing components are reused.
- Fully revealed slides fit desktop and small viewports.
- Mermaid diagrams have reading direction and a conclusion.
- The repository's package-manager build passes when dependencies are available.
